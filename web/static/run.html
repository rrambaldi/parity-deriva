reading the chart

what the marks mean · what a click, the wheel and a drag do · times are UTC

esc
  • every trade is marked with an arrow saying which way it was taken, and a click anywhere on the chart zooms onto the nearest one - as does a click on the capital curve, which goes to the trade that made that step
  • hovering a bar reads out its four prices and how far it travelled, high to low, in pips
  • the wheel zooms the time and dragging moves along it; dragging the time axis at the bottom stretches it too
  • the wheel over the prices on the left, or with shift held, zooms the prices; dragging the price axis stretches them, dragging the chart then moves them up and down too, and a double click on the price axis fits them to the bars again
  • the crosshair tags the price and the time under the pointer on the axes
  • shift and a drag measures: pips, percent, bars and time from where the drag started; it stays until the next press or esc
  • ctrl (cmd on a Mac) and a drag draws a box, and the chart zooms onto its bars and its prices
  • keys: shift + ← → pan, + and − zoom, home and end go to either end of the run, f fits the prices, esc shows the whole range; ← → on their own still walk the runs of a set, ↑ ↓ the trades
  • 1D 1W 1M 3M show that much time, ending where the chart does
  • under the chart: how many high and medium calendar events of the run's currencies are on show, and "calendar events" draws them - solid for high, dashed for medium - and names a bar's events in the readout when hovered
  • and under that the selected trade's card: its number and id, side, outcome, P&L and pips, then entry, exit, stop and target in the colours of their marks - the chart fits the candles, so a stop or a target outside them is only here, marked off the chart - and the calendar's events while it was open
  • the grip right under the chart makes it taller or shorter: drag it, or focus it and press ↑ ↓; a double click gives the chart back its own height, and this browser remembers the one chosen
  • the strip under the chart is the whole run's capital with the stretch on show boxed: drag the box to move, its edges to widen or narrow it, press anywhere else to go there
  • the selected trade gets an arrow from its entry to its exit, coloured by what it made, and a box around the candles its entry rule read
  • support and resistance are bands, not lines: the width is what decides that two swings are one level and that a bar touched it, and each is drawn from the bar that confirmed it and never from the swing itself
  • the strip right under the candles has the curves that are not on the price axis, and the one below it has the levels on their own
  • times are UTC
  • zooming in past a point redraws the same window on a finer series - the candles change timeframe, the trades and the curves do not: they stay the strategy's, placed by time and stepped across the finer bars
  • the orders were already filled on the finest series the store holds, whatever the strategy read
  • the thin whiskers on a zoomed chart are the ask high and the bid low, which are the prices the fill rule reads
