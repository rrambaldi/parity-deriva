reading the chart
  • every trade is marked with an arrow saying which way it was taken, and a click anywhere on the chart zooms onto the nearest one - as does a click on the capital curve, which goes to the trade that made that step
  • hovering a bar reads out its four prices and how far it travelled, high to low, in pips
  • the wheel zooms and dragging moves sideways once the chart is zoomed
  • the selected trade gets an arrow from its entry to its exit, coloured by what it made, and a box around the candles its entry rule read
  • support and resistance are bands, not lines: the width is what decides that two swings are one level and that a bar touched it, and each is drawn from the bar that confirmed it and never from the swing itself
  • the strip right under the candles has the curves that are not on the price axis, and the one below it has the levels on their own
  • times are UTC
  • zooming in past a point redraws the same window on a finer series - the candles change timeframe, the trades and the curves do not: they stay the strategy's, placed by time and stepped across the finer bars
  • the orders were already filled on the finest series the store holds, whatever the strategy read
  • the thin whiskers on a zoomed chart are the ask high and the bid low, which are the prices the fill rule reads
